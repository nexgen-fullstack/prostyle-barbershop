Our story

Years behind the chair. Now, his own place.

Pro Style is Alvaro Wolf’s dream come true: after years of cutting hair in Madeira and building a loyal clientele, he opened his own shop on Avenida da Madalena in Funchal.

The craft is taken seriously, the vibe is relaxed: good music, coffee, PlayStation and a team that knows your name. From kids to grandads, everyone leaves in style.

FAQ

Everything you need to know.

Do I need an appointment at Pro Style Barbershop?

We recommend booking online on Booksy to secure your time, but walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is free.

How much is a haircut in Funchal at Pro Style?

A simple cut is €15, a fade €17 and the hair & beard combo €24. See the full price list above.

What are the opening hours?

We’re open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 19:00. Closed on Sundays.

Where is Pro Style Barbershop?

Av. da Madalena 101, Fração B, 9020-330 Funchal, Madeira, Portugal. Tap “Get directions” to open the route in Google Maps.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. We cut hair for clients of all ages — lots of dads bring their sons along.

Do you do colouring, highlights and platinum?

Yes. Colouring from €35, highlights/lowlights €60 and platinum (snow blonde) €70.
